English

  1. Send an email to erovon2@gmail.com with the subject "KOZIRI data deletion".
  2. Send it from the email linked to your Google or Facebook account, or include your nickname in the game.
  3. We delete your account, nickname, avatar, progress and coins within 30 days and reply when it is done.

To remove KOZIRI's access to Facebook: Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → KOZIRI → Remove. For Google: myaccount.google.com/connections.

Русский

  1. Напишите на erovon2@gmail.com с темой «KOZIRI data deletion».
